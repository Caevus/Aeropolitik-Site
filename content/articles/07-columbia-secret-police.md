Quiet air fills the streets of Orange Bay, the first stillness in many weeks. This is not a peaceful calm, however, as Columbians huddle in their homes and businesses waiting in anxious anticipation for what might come next. While the soldiers, for the most part, are no longer in the streets, the Bragg Regime maintains its grip through more sinister means: a web of sleuths, informants, and agents that have been consolidated under the "Bureau of Domestic Security".

The influence of this agency, which amongst the diaspora has been nicknamed "Domesec", can be found far outside of Orange Bay. From Callumet to New Philippi and elsewhere, Domesec seems to have entrenched itself deeply. Reports from those in Columbia mention anonymous "watch leaders" being appointed over neighborhoods and companies. These agents, it seems, are working to snuff out dissent and stifle any remaining freedoms and independence those living under Bragg's rule may have.
