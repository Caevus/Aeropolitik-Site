In its second plenary session of the month, the Free Cities Congress continued the debate over allocation of funds and manpower toward the Concordan Relief and Reconstruction Fund. Debate over the Fund, established two years ago near the end of the war, has been increasingly intense as the legally mandated renewal date approaches. Tensions are inflamed over the Fund's scope and cost, which has left its future in purgatory.

Two major camps have merged in the latest session, neither with a majority. Those who support increasing the Fund's size, lead by Delegate Richard Modesto (IP&numsp;-&numsp;Aedem), assert that the responsibilities and financing around Concorda's reconstruction are best directed centrally through Congress. Delegate Wysteria Parks (CTP&numsp;-&numsp;Helena) and others in opposition have argued that the scope of the Fund must be limited to reduce waste and ensure costs are kept low. Instead, they say that financing beyond the Fund's current size is best coordinated through individual member cities according to need and means.
