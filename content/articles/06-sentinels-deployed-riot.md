As protests boiled into riots on the Old Foundry Deck late Saturday, many were surprised to see the usual patrolman line augmented with the bronze helmets of the Sentinel Corps. Twenty-two Sentinels in total were deployed to assist the Patrol with crowd control and dispersal, a first in the Corps' history. As the dust settles, a fierce debate is coming to the forefront about the role of the Sentinels. Some are asking: Are they saviors of the people, or merely servants to those in power?

Sources say Mayor Clifton had been preparing the unprecedented measure for weeks, as unrest across the city has grown more potent. In coordination with Commissioner Dreyfus and Police Chief Erikson, fifty Sentinels so-far have been trained in riot tactics and mob disarmament, with another hundred slated for the training in the coming months. As part of their deployment, the Sentinels have been issued appropriate riot gear and batons.
