In a pomp filled ceremony Wednesday, the Sentinel Corps celebrated a number of their ranks. The most notable of these was an award for Valor given to green Corps member Edwin Leyendecker.

Before bestowing the medal upon Leyendecker's neck, Commissioner Dreyfus regailed the rookie's short but exceptional tenure. From stopping an armed robbery without the use of force, to assisting Carolinian refugees after the terrible complex fire, to the latest event—the one for which he has received his award—where he valiantly responded to an emergency call from one of his fellow Sentinels. While the Sentinel, Oscar Wallace, ultimately perished from his gunshot wounds, Leyendecker's quick response and strategic thinking prevented further death and uncovered key evidence which has allowed the Corps to pursue the main suspect of this heneious act of murder.
