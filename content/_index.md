## Aeropolitik

**Aeropolitik** is a series of vignettes, stories, and artistic pieces which explore politics and conflict in a world of floating aerocities focused around the metropolis of Helena. 

As the summer of 1938 arrives, storm clouds gather on the horizon. In Helena, still fresh from war, daily rituals carry an air of tension—Sentinels glide overhead on watchful patrols, market bustling is interrupted by heated arguments, crowds gather around newsboys shouting the latest polls, and the whole city hums with an undercurrent of dread. A vicious election season, economic fragility, political incompetence, and ideological clashing threaten to snap the threads barely holding the city together. The fate of Helena—keystone of the Free Cities—may well determine the path of the entire world.

Amid the uncertainty, those willing to push back against destructive forces face incredible challenges: backroom deals in smoke-filled rooms, citizens flock to tabloids and tub-thumpers, and many lament the insurmountable task of changing anything for the better. Corruption, complacency, and cynicism hold an iron grip on Helena’s political scene. Those who see a brighter future for the city are few in number and desperate for the will to take action.

Even in a gilded world, hope can still be found, but only if enough can see the sunrays amidst the storm.
